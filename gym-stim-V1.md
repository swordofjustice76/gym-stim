# Gym Stim — V1 lock (11 Sep 2026)

Chamber plan after the twist-index + sniff-cap pass. Design work starts here. Do not tool the rejected options.

## Architecture
V1 = **Architecture 1 body + Architecture 2 feel.**
38 mm flashlight stack. Knurl, detents, clip, clicks on the outside. Not a visible modular GoPro stack.

- Lite / Mid / Elite share the same tube family
- Keep: blank cap so a no-salts Elite silhouette is the same body
- Kill for V1: Architecture 3 visible modules, fourth chamber
- Body quote stays **38 × 190**. Wider/shorter options, Y-jam, carousel counts: `gym-stim-sizing.md`. Do not tool 44 × 145 or a carousel until that file is picked.

## Chamber A — top puck (bayonet)
**Elite ships as a sniff puck. Same latch takes a chalk puck later.**
- Quarter-turn / bayonet top, ~22 mm stack, 38 mm OD. Not a junk-drawer screw cap
- **Salt puck (V1):** 3–4 ml NH3 well, twist port, recessed `NH3`. Body never touches ammonia
- **Chalk puck (accessory SKU):** compressed MgCO3 wafer **32 mm × 12 mm**, ~6–8 g, rub face. No ammonia in this puck.
- Do not put salt and chalk in one well. Swap the puck, do not share air
- Mid / Lite: blank puck or chalk puck on the same latch
Kill for V1: flip hinge, turret, child-lock, food/discs/tape in the NH3 puck

## Chamber B — electrolyte clicker
**Electrolyte Fastchew-class discs only.**
- Tablet ~18 × 5.5 mm. Cassette OD ~22 mm.
- B6 cassette + B3 one-click gate
- 8 tabs locked. 44 mm stack + 12 mm spring + 6 mm gate = **62 mm Chamber B**
- Caffeine stays in C
Kill for V1: cubes, gum, Nuun, dual-flavour magazine

## Chamber C — twist-index powder
**3-cell carrier. Twist collar 1/2/3. Twist carrier off to refill.**
- Elite / Mid: 3 × ~22–25 ml
- BYO powder
Kill for V1: single flask as the only C

## One full SKU — Gym Stim Elite
38 × 190. A 22 / B 62 / C ~90. Empty box launch.

See `gym-stim-sizing.md` before changing diameter.
