# Gym Stim — product brief for launch planning
15 September 2026. For Buildpad / go-to-market structure. Not a full engineering ICD.

**Owner:** Damon (swordofjustice76), Brisbane, SEQ, Australia  
**Repo (private design):** swordofjustice76/gym-stim  

---

## One-line

Gym Stim is a modular aluminium stick that carries what a gym-goer needs **before and during** a session — powder, and optional salts / chalk / chews / small dry items — without going back to the locker.

---

## What it is

A **44 mm × 175 mm** 6061-T6 tube with three bays on one spine:

| Bay | Role | What the user does |
|---|---|---|
| **A** (nose) | Swap puck | Sniffing salts **or** chalk **or** blank |
| **B** (mid) | Swap pack | Chew clicker **or** dry tin **or** combined A+B storage |
| **C** (tail) | Always powder | Three ~32 ml cells. Fill from their own tub. Dump one cell into a bottle |

**Sold as modular hardware:** one body, swap the guts. Not three separate tubes the user screws together.

**Launch (Australia):** empty metal and empty inserts only. No food, no ammonia fill, no branded powder in the box. User brings their own powder and optional chews / gum / strips.

---

## Who it is for

- People who train in a commercial gym or home gym and already buy pre-workout, creatine, or intra powder.
- They want **one-hand access mid-set** (clipped on bag, belt, or held with the bottle).
- They are tired of juggling a tub, a separate chalk bag, a chew bottle, and a pocket full of gum.

**Not** primarily for:

- Ultra / race-day endurance kits (different form factor)
- People who only use a shaker and nothing else
- A “medicine cabinet” or pill organiser

Ritual: **pre-session fill → during-session dump / click / tip → no locker trip.**

---

## Problem it solves

| Pain | How Gym Stim answers |
|---|---|
| Scooping powder at the gym from a big tub | Three pre-loaded cells in C; select, unlock door, invert, scrape |
| Chalk or salts in a separate bag | A-puck on the same stick |
| Electrolyte / glucose chews in a rattly tube | Pack D: one disc per click |
| Gum, strips, earplugs, a ring in pockets | Pack T: press-and-tip dry tin |
| Buying a fixed “all-in-one” that doesn’t match how they train | Swap puck and mid-pack; C stays powder |

## Product system (what ships)

### Core SKU — Elite (default)

- Aluminium body + clip  
- One A puck (blank or empty sniff)  
- One B pack — **Pack T (dry tin)** preferred for v1 gym-goer; Pack D (chew clicker) as alternative / later  
- Empty C (three cells)  
- Wipe card / desiccant as needed  

Copy: *Same stick. Swap the puck, the mid pack, the powder.*

### Accessories (separate SKUs)

| SKU | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Sniff puck | Smelling salts well (user or later factory fill; AU launch empty) |
| Chalk puck | Compressed MgCO₃ wafer seat |
| Blank puck | Closed nose |
| Pack D | Dual-well chew dispenser (8 × ~18 mm hard discs, 4+4, I/II ring) |
| Pack T | ~35 ml dry tin, press-hold-tip, spring-closed door |
| Pack AB | A+B storage liner (~50–55 ml); A bayonet cap is the lid |
| Extra C cells / cartridge | Refill powder module |

### Flask config

Same body + Pack AB + empty C. Storage nose + powder tail. No second extrusion.

---

## How each bay is used

### C — powder (always)

1. Unscrew C, fill three cells from their tub (top fill).  
2. At the bottle: select cell 1 / 2 / 3 → unlock rotary door → invert → pull paddle if needed → lock door.  
3. ~32 ml per cell ≈ one session’s fluffy pre or a creatine scoop — not a 22 g mega-scoop in one cell.

Owner’s own pre brand (“Flood”) is **not** in production for a long time. C is BYO only at launch.

### A — nose

Bayonet swap: sniff **or** chalk **or** blank. Ammonia never shares a well with food. Body never stores ammonia; only the puck does.

### B — mid

| Pack | Mid-set action | Typical contents |
|---|---|---|
| **D** | Click → one hard disc in palm | Electrolyte / glucose / caffeine **discs** that fit Ø17.4–18.4 × 5–6 mm |
| **T** | Press button, tip, release | Wrapped gum, caffeine strips (e.g. Revvies-class), mints, earplugs, hair ties |
| **AB** | Cap off to refill; optional side door | Larger dry storage; replaces A+B |

Same body button hole: Pack D = stiff clicker stroke; Pack T = short soft door stroke (deadman — closes on release so C pour cannot empty the tin).

---

## What it is not

- Not a filled supplement product at AU launch (avoids food + therapeutic goods complexity in the carton)  
- Not a Hydralyte / Nuun / gummy / gum **dispenser** (wrong shape for Pack D)  
- Not a peptide / vial case (optional BYO in AB only; not a claim)  
- Not three body segments the customer assembles  
- Not a second official caffeine product stacked on a future in-house pre (Flood is far out; BYO stack is the user’s problem)

## Positioning

| | |
|---|---|
| Category | Gym / EDC hardware — modular carry system |
| Premium cue | Aluminium, anodise, mechanical click / door, clip |
| Habit | Fills once at home or locker; uses between sets |
| Refill revenue | Later: pucks, packs, optional food sleeves (electrolyte / glucose food; caffeine sleeve only after ARTG if sold as such) |
| Price architecture | Hardware first; accessories and sleeves second |

Competing substitutes today: plastic pill tubes, chalk bags, tub in the locker, pocket trash. Gym Stim’s job is **one clipped object** for the session.

---

## Launch constraints (Australia)

1. **Empty hardware only** in the first carton.  
2. No medical / therapeutic claims on the stick.  
3. Food-contact materials on powder and chew paths; validate before any filled sleeve.  
4. Caffeine as a Gym Stim–branded chew only after regulatory path (ARTG if required); BYO discs/strips/gum are user choice.  
5. Manufacturing: NDA before CAD; first proto = body + Pack T door; Pack D and C seal after.

---

## Success metrics (suggested for Buildpad structure)

- Unit: one stick sold, configured, and used for a full session without a locker return  
- Attachment: % buyers who add a second pack or puck within 90 days  
- Refill: C cells / sleeves reorder rate (post food SKUs)  
- Support: low “doesn’t fit my chews / powder” tickets → caliper education and disc-band honesty  

---

## Open decisions for launch plan (not engineering)

- Lead mid-pack at launch: **T vs D** (recommend T for broader gym-goer; D for chew enthusiasts)  
- Sniff puck in box vs blank only (AU empty + retail optics)  
- Price ladder: Elite vs body-only vs accessory bundles  
- Channels: D2C first vs gym retail  
- Timeline: CNC proto → pilot 300–500 → wider

## One paragraph you can paste

Gym Stim is a pocket- and clip-sized aluminium modular stick for gym-goers. One tube holds a swap-in nose (salts or chalk), a swap-in mid pack (either a one-disc chew clicker or a press-and-tip dry tin), and a three-cell powder flask the user fills from their own tub. Australian launch is empty hardware only. The product is built around the real ritual of pre- and mid-session use: no locker trip, one hand, same stick every day. Accessories and optional food sleeves come later; the body and interfaces stay fixed.
