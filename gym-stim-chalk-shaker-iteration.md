# Chalk module iteration — 15 September 2026

**Status:** Locked over rub-face wafer and drop-out cylinder.

## Mechanism
BYO chalk shaker puck on A bayonet.
- Empty at launch; user fills crushed/loose MgCO₃ (~8–15 ml).
- Rotating face disc: CLOSED (hard detent + seal) ↔ OPEN (scatter holes ~1.0–1.5 mm).
- Shake onto hands; click CLOSED before pocket.
- Optional under-disc screen.

## Killed for chalk
- 32×12 mm rub wafer
- Ejectable solid cylinder (custom SKU)
- Liquid chalk
- Shared well with ammonia
