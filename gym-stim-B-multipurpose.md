# Gym Stim Elite — Chamber B, a little more multipurpose
12 September 2026. Addendum to `gym-stim-B-redesign.md`. Does not replace the shuttle.

---

## The constraint

B cannot become “any pill.” The shuttle is a one-size gate. Soft, fat, tall, or dissolving tabs jam it.

A little more multipurpose means:

> **Same 40 mm bay. Same button. Same window. Broader disc family. Two wells that can disagree. Optional collars for smaller lozenges.**

It does not mean a second mechanism, a tray of mixed pharmacy, or Nuun.

---

## What changes

### 1. B is a bay, not a flavour

Split the part:

- **B-shell** — stays in the aluminium. Button, return spring, window cheek, I/II ring detents, extract path after C unscrews.
- **B-pack** — the removable cassette. Food-contact. Keyed. 40 mm tall. This is the SKU.

Launch ships **Pack D** (disc). The shell never knows what flavour is inside.

Daily can reuse Pack D in a plastic shell later. That is the other half of multipurpose.

### 2. Disc family, not one exact die

Nominal stay **18.0 × 5.5 mm**.

Acceptance band the shuttle must swallow after a humid session:

| | Min | Nominal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diameter | 17.4 mm | 18.0 | 18.4 |
| Thickness | 5.0 mm | 5.5 | 6.0 |

Shuttle pocket becomes **18.6 × 6.2 mm** with a radiused lead-in and a closed roof. Window stays **18.5 × 7 mm** at the cheek; the disc still presents 2–3 mm proud.

Why a band, not “whatever”:

- Fastchews are dime-wide (~17.9 mm) and batch-swell.
- A 16 mm mint falls into the pocket sideways without a collar.
- A 19–20 mm Nuun does not enter the well. Do not grow the well to chase it. Two 20 mm wells plus a septum do not fit a 40 mm ID.

Caliper a real Fastchew before the pocket is frozen. The band is a process window, not a promise that chemist-shop glucose fits.

### 3. Wells may disagree (BYO only)

Official branded sleeves stay **one formula per pack** so the 2.9.4 label is one NIP.

The empty launch cassette and the blank BYO sleeve may be loaded mixed:

```text
Well I  = electrolyte discs
Well II = glucose discs
```

Same band. Same thickness. Ring I / II is now a *payload* selector, not just a refill selector.

That is the actual user-facing multipurpose: mid-set electrolytes, then twist and take a carb, without unscrewing C.

Do not sell a factory “half and half” sleeve. Labelling becomes two foods in one article.

### 4. Snap-in collars for the 16 mm class

Optional well inserts. Drop into an empty well. Neck **18.2 → 16.2 mm**.

Use when the user has a 16 mm mint / small lozenge that would otherwise stand on edge.

Rules:

- Collar height = four discs + follower face. It cannot ride up into the shuttle.
- Collar is a different colour from the pack so you can see it is fitted.
- Lost collar → pack reverts to 18 mm family. No extra parts in the tube.
- Do not stack a collar on a 18 mm disc. Hard stop at the well mouth.

Collars are an accessory bag in the box, not a second mechanism.

### 5. What still does not fit

| Still no | Why |
|---|---|
| Nuun / 5 g dissolve tabs | Diameter and job. Needs 500 ml of water. |
| Tic Tac / loose beans | Too small. Rattle. Open hopper. |
| Gum, gummies, cubes | Squash in the pocket. |
| Size 0 / 00 capsules | 23 mm long. Window is 18.5. Wrong shape. |
| Swallow tablets as a named fit | Thickness lottery + TGA language if caffeinated. |
| Powder in B | That is C. |
| Two mechanisms in one pack | Button would lie. |

If they need capsules, that is a later **Pack P** with its own pusher geometry and a wider window cut. Do not tool Pack P until Pack D clicks dry and damp. Do not cut a bigger window in the aluminium “just in case.”

## Pack map (do not launch all of this)

| Pack | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **D — disc** | Launch | 4+4, isolated wells, shuttle, band above |
| **D blank** | Launch carton | Empty Pack D + two collars in a bag |
| **D electrolyte / glucose / mint / Mg** | Refill SKUs | One formula each |
| **T — tray** | Not V2 | Individual docks, mixed shapes. Different pusher. Parked. |
| **P — capsule** | Not V2 | Needs a new cheek. Parked. |

Multipurpose at launch is Pack D + band + mixed BYO wells + collars. That is enough.

## Mechanism changes to Pack D (only)

Keep from the B redesign:

- Isolated wells. No Y. No priority throat. No per-click 180° rotor.
- Ring I / II parks the armed well on the shuttle.
- Followers to a ceiling.
- Dead click when the armed well is empty.
- Cassette out after C unscrews.

Change:

- Pocket 18.6 × 6.2 (was 18.4 × 5.8).
- Follower face cupped ~0.4 mm so a 17.4 mm disc stays centred.
- Well mouth accepts a collar.
- Ring markings can read as payload marks on a BYO pack (`I` / `II` is enough; do not print flavour names on the aluminium).

Height budget still 40 mm. The extra 0.4 mm pocket thickness comes out of the follower, not out of C.

---

## Law and label

- Launch pack empty.
- Official sleeve = one food, one NIP, one 2.9.4 carton.
- Stick copy: “chew magazine for 17.4–18.4 × 5–6 mm discs.” Compatible-with list on the card.
- Mixed BYO is the user’s assembly. You do not claim two formulas.
- Caffeine disc still ARTG-only. Not launch.

## Prototype add-ons

After Prototype A/B from the B redesign (dry + swollen 18 × 5.5):

### Prototype E — band

Print slugs at the four corners of the band (17.4 × 5.0, 17.4 × 6.0, 18.4 × 5.0, 18.4 × 6.0). Fifty clicks each. If any corner double-feeds or stands on edge, shrink the band before you print “compatible with.”

### Prototype F — mixed wells

Well I 18.0 × 5.5, well II 17.6 × 5.2. Prove the ring change does not let well II crumbs into well I.

### Prototype G — collar

16.2 mm slugs, 5.5 mm thick, four high. Collar in, collar out, collar forgotten with an 18 mm disc on top. The forgotten-collar case must refuse to close or refuse to click, not shave a disc.

---

## Design intent

> **Click is still click.**
>
> **I and II can be two jobs.**
>
> **Smaller lozenges need a collar, not a new stick.**
>
> **Bigger / softer / wetter things do not get a vote.**

That is a little more multipurpose. It is not a pharmacy.
