# The Gym Stim — product brief
Held: 10 September 2026
Updated: 11 September 2026 — Elite is a 38×190 mm container (salt cap / chew cassette / 70 ml BYO flask). Launch box empty.
Status: parked concept — user called it a real winner

Source of truth: github.com/swordofjustice76/gym-stim

## Problem
Pre-workout is bulky and annoying: tub, scoop, shaker, 400 ml mix, wait time.
Goal: streamline getting ready at the gym door. The stick is the container. Pair with the water bottle and the pre-workout tub they already own.
Not a protein/amino vape. Not a slim pen that hides 6 g citrulline. Not a locked-in Flood-only hopper.

## Line split
Two products. One consumable spine.

| | **Gym Stim Elite** | **Gym Stim Daily** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Strength rooms, powerlifting, serious boxes | Chains, 24/7s, F45-style, clubs that will not touch ammonia |
| Hardware | 38×190 mm aluminium container | No ammonia. Plastic chew stick later, or no stick at launch |
| Chamber A | Smelling-salt + menthol cap | Does not exist |
| Chamber B | 10-chew magazine | Same chews, sleeve / optional plastic magazine |
| Chamber C | 70 ml BYO powder flask | No flask — Daily is food only |
| Consumables | Salt caps + chews. Flood optional | Flood sachets + same chews |
| Door price | $34–44 loaded stick | $12–18 sleeve box (5 Flood + 1 chew sleeve) |
| Feel | Cold metal, clip, weight | Soft pack, reception tray, no poison mark |

Do not put ammonia in a cheaper body and call it Daily. Chains do not want a mild version of Elite. They want a different product that happens to refill from the same carton.

Do not launch two Flood formulas. One powder, one chew, two doors.

## Gym Stim Elite

Reusable aluminium **three-chamber container**. The stick is the product. Flood is an optional fill, not the lock-in.

People already bought a tub. Chamber C unscrews, they funnel *their* pre-workout in at home, and they carry salts + chews + powder in one object. Branded Flood exists so a buyer with no tub still has something to put in C, and so gyms have a refill SKU.

**Locked size (BYO powder)**
- **38 mm diameter × 190 mm long**
- 6061-T6, type II anodise, pocket clip
- Loaded weight ~140–180 g depending on their powder
- Cap = A. Mid body = B magazine. Lower body = removable C flask
- C flask volume **70 ml** with fill lines at 20 / 45 / 70 ml

Why 70 ml: typical tub scoops run 6–15 g. Fluffy powder sits ~0.50–0.60 g/ml. 70 ml is about **three average scoops** or **four small C4-style scoops**. A 25 g “serious” scoop only fits two serves — print that on the flask. Do not chase Pre-Jym 26 g × 4. That is a tub, not a pocket.

**Chamber A — Snap (salts) — factory refill cap**
- 3–4 ml well in the twist cap
- Dry / unactivated
- Menthol-forward
- Poison / warning mark on the cap
- They buy replacement caps. They do not homebrew ammonia into A

**Chamber B — Chew (electrolytes) — factory sleeve**
- 10 × 16 mm tabs, ~55 mm magazine
- Click one into the palm
- Caffeine-free
- They can run it empty. Chews are the upsell, not required for the stick to work

**Chamber C — BYO powder flask — 3–4 gym days**
- Screw-off flask, 70 ml, wide mouth so a tub scoop actually fits
- Dump hatch at the bottle end: open, tip into *their* water bottle, close
- Fill lines, not a precision meter — their Ghost and their C4 are not the same density
- Optional funnel cap in the box
- Optional branded Flood sachet: tear into C at home if they have no tub
- Desiccant puck in the flask lid. Wipe and dry before refill or it cakes
- Not four tiny cells. People will not portion Ghost into four 17 mm barrels on a Sunday

Stack inside 38 × 190:
- A cap ~22 mm
- B magazine ~55 mm
- C flask ~90 mm (70 ml at ~32 mm bore is ~87 mm of column)
- Hatch / seals / clip end ~23 mm

Aluminium is the point. Plastic Elite is a different product.

**Elite session**
1. Sunday: unscrew C, scoop their tub to a line (or tear Flood in)
2. At the gym: dump one flask-tip into the bottle — or dump the day’s line
3. Heavy set → snap A
4. Mid-session → chew from B
5. Home: wipe C, refill when empty. Buy salt caps and chew sleeves when those run out

**Elite channel**
- Brisbane / GC strength gyms, APL rooms, boxes that already sell salts
- Consignment counter tray
- Owner margin ~40–50%
- Impulse is the stick. Annuity is Flood + chew + salt-cap refills

## Gym Stim Daily

Chain-safe door fuel. No ammonia, no poison mark, no “nose bleach” culture.

Launch Daily as **consumables only**:
- Carton of 5 Flood packs
- One chew sleeve
- Optional plastic chew puck / stick only if the Elite magazine insert tools once and a cheap shell is a second shot of the same mould
- Menthol inhaler is not Daily. Menthol-without-ammonia still looks like salts to a club manager

Daily is what you can put next to the protein bars at Anytime / Goodlife / a 24/7 without a staff argument. It will sit next to MixMate in some rooms. That is fine. MixMate is a mixed drink. Daily is dump-in-your-own-bottle plus a chew.

**Daily session**
1. Fill bottle → tear Flood in
2. Mid-session → one chew from the sleeve
3. After → finish bottle

**Daily channel**
- Reception tray, vending coil, or a small counter box
- Gym margin still ~40%
- Chain conversations are “member amenity + margin,” not “our elite sniff stick”
- Powder-only box exists if a club will not even do chews

If a chain wants hardware later, sell them the plastic Chamber B only. Never Chamber A.

## Shared spine (do not fork)

### Flood (optional later, one formula)
- Same mix: ~6–8 g L-citrulline + nitrate + sodium + flavour + 100–150 mg caffeine
- Default fill for Elite C is the user’s own tub
- Flood exists as a sachet so a no-tub buyer can pour it into C, and so Daily has a SKU
- Under 5% caffeine, max 200 mg/day from the product as sports food
- Formula stays one SKU

### Chew sleeve
- Same tablet, same diameter, same caffeine-free rule
- Elite loads it into Chamber B
- Daily sells the sleeve as the chew
- Launch flavour: one (citrus or watermelon)

### Reload (home, optional, both lines)
- Daily creatine puck (~5 g)
- Not carried to the gym
- Not required to launch either door kit

## What we rejected
- Protein vape / inhaling whey
- Putting Flood gel or 32 g loose powder inside a slim pen
- Four tiny factory cells people must portion at home — C is one 70 ml flask
- Requiring branded Flood for the stick to work
- Mixing ammonia into the drink or tank
- Dummy micro-doses of citrulline in a 2 ml cartridge
- Open gumball-style chew hopper
- Caffeine in both Flood and the chew at launch
- A “lite Elite” that still has ammonia in plastic
- Two different pre-workout powders at launch
- Leading the chain pitch with the word Stim if the buyer flinches — pack can say Flood on the Daily carton

## Australia sell path

Launch SKU is the **empty stick**. One object, one regime: a reusable sports container. Not a food. Not a medicine. Not a poison pack.

Do not ship Chamber A charged, do not pre-load chews, do not drop a Flood sachet in the box. That single kit would mix three laws on one barcode:
- empty aluminium body = consumer goods / sports accessory
- smelling salts = warning / Poisons Standard territory (ammonia is scheduled by concentration; gym salts already sell as their own SKU)
- Flood and chews = Standard 2.9.4 formulated supplementary sports food (mandatory not-for-under-15 / pregnant, NIP, directions, one-day quantity)
- tablets + performance claims = TGA / ARTG risk

Box at launch: empty Elite body, empty C flask, funnel, desiccant puck, wipe card, “fill C from your own tub” instructions. Soft claims only: carries powder, clicker, cap well. No 1RM language. No “inhaled pre-workout.”

Refills come later as **separate products**, separate labels, customer assembles:
- Salt cap — own poison/warning label, own SKU
- Chew sleeve — own 2.9.4 sports-food label
- Flood sachet — own 2.9.4 sports-food label
- Daily carton never touches a salt cap

Compatible-with list on the card, not a bundled multipack. Gym tray can sit the empty stick next to someone else’s salts and chews until your refills exist.

## Why two products, not one watered-down stick
Elite is a strength object. Daily is a reception food.
Same Flood and chews mean one blender, one tablet press, two cartons.
Chains will not put ammonia next to the kids’ holiday-pass flyer. Independents will not pay aluminium money for a plastic stick with the snap deleted.
Bike bar-end dispensers already exist. A gym-pocket aluminium two-chamber stick does not. That is Elite. Daily exists so the consumable spine can live in rooms Elite will never enter.
