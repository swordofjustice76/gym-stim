# The Gym Stim — product brief
Held: 10 September 2026
Updated: 11 September 2026 — Elite is a 30×150 mm three-chamber stick (salt / 10 chews / 4 Flood pucks)
Status: parked concept — user called it a real winner

Source of truth: github.com/swordofjustice76/gym-stim

## Problem
Pre-workout is bulky and annoying: tub, scoop, shaker, 400 ml mix, wait time.
Goal: streamline getting ready at the gym door. Pair with the water bottle people already carry.
Not a protein/amino vape. Not one slim pen that hides 6 g citrulline.

## Line split
Two products. One consumable spine.

| | **Gym Stim Elite** | **Gym Stim Daily** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Strength rooms, powerlifting, serious boxes | Chains, 24/7s, F45-style, clubs that will not touch ammonia |
| Hardware | 30×150 mm aluminium, three chambers | No ammonia. Plastic chew stick later, or no stick at launch |
| Chamber A | Smelling-salt + menthol cap | Does not exist |
| Chamber B | 10-chew magazine | Same chews, sleeve / optional plastic magazine |
| Chamber C | Four 8 g Flood pucks | No — Daily keeps Flood as sachets |
| Consumables | Puck + chew + salt-cap refills | **Same Flood formula** as sachets + same chews |
| Door price | $34–44 loaded stick | $12–18 sleeve box (5 Flood + 1 chew sleeve) |
| Feel | Cold metal, clip, weight | Soft pack, reception tray, no poison mark |

Do not put ammonia in a cheaper body and call it Daily. Chains do not want a mild version of Elite. They want a different product that happens to refill from the same carton.

Do not launch two Flood formulas. One powder, one chew, two doors.

## Gym Stim Elite

Reusable aluminium **three-chamber** stick. All three materials ride in the body. A slim pen cannot hold four real Flood doses — the body is a fat marker, not a vape.

**Locked size**
- **30 mm diameter × 150 mm long**
- 6061-T6, type II anodise, pocket clip
- Carry weight loaded ~90–120 g
- One twist cap = Chamber A. Body stack = B then C. No loose powder in an open hopper

**Chamber A — Snap (salts) — few weeks**
- Smelling-salt + menthol in the cap well
- Volume **3–4 ml** (does not need more)
- Dry / unactivated fill so it can travel
- Menthol-forward
- Poison / warning mark on the cap

**Chamber B — Chew (electrolytes) — 4–6 sessions**
- Magazine of **10** Fastchew-format tabs (~16 mm × 5–6 mm)
- Stack height ~55 mm, 18 mm bore
- One-handed click drops one chew into the palm
- Caffeine-free. Flood stays the caffeine source
- Tamper-resistant insert. Same insert can later drop into a Daily plastic shell

**Chamber C — Flood (pre-workout) — 4 sessions**
- **Four pressed 8 g pucks**, not a powder hopper
- Each puck = one bottle dump (citrulline + nitrate + sodium + caffeine + flavour)
- Puck ~20 mm × 10 mm. Four-shot stack + gate ~55 mm
- Push / twist indexes one puck. Drop into the bottle they already carry
- Loose 32 g of powder in four vials does not fit this diameter. Pucks are the only way C stays inside 30 × 150
- If a puck press is too hard for v1: step the body to **38 mm** and use four sealed powder cells (~10 mm of column per 8 g at that bore). Do not go back to a slim pen

Aluminium is the point. Plastic Elite is a different product.

**Elite session (one load = ~4 gym days)**
1. Index one C puck into the bottle
2. First heavy set → snap A
3. Mid-session → one chew from B
4. Repeat next session. After four pucks, buy a refill kit (4 pucks + chew sleeve + salt cap)

**Elite channel**
- Brisbane / GC strength gyms, APL rooms, boxes that already sell salts
- Consignment counter tray
- Owner margin ~40–50%
- Impulse is the stick. Annuity is Flood + chew + salt-cap refills
