# The Gym Stim — product brief
Held: 10 September 2026
Updated: 10 September 2026 — electrolyte chew chamber added
Status: parked concept — user called it a real winner

## Problem
Pre-workout is bulky and annoying: tub, scoop, shaker, 400 ml mix, wait time.
Goal: streamline getting ready at the gym door. Pair with the water bottle people already carry.
Not a protein/amino vape. Not one slim pen that hides 6 g citrulline.

## Product (three pieces, one kit)

### 1. Stim Stick (pocket, two chambers)
Reusable pocket stick. Two separate chambers, one body.

**Chamber A — Snap (salts)**
- Smelling-salt + menthol
- Walk-up snap before a heavy set
- Menthol-forward so it does not wreck a shared gym
- Launch safer in AU: this chamber = salts only

**Chamber B — Chew (electrolytes) — was missing**
- Sealed magazine of electrolyte chews (SaltStick Fastchew format: ~16 mm tablet)
- Capacity: 6–8 chews per load
- One-handed click / twist drops one chew into the palm
- Mid-set salts without a second powder dump or a fridge drink
- Buccal absorb; works if they have already started the set and will not stop to mix
- Refill sleeve is the labelled food product. Empty stick is just the dispenser.
- Launch flavour: one (citrus or watermelon). Caffeine-free chews so Flood stays the only caffeine source.
- Do not pour loose unpackaged chews into an open hopper. Unit tablets in a tamper-resistant magazine only.

Optional later on the same stick: caffeine/theanine pellet or gum as Chamber C. Not launch.

### 2. Flood (dump in their existing bottle)
- Single-serve powder stick pack
- ~6–8 g L-citrulline + nitrate + sodium + flavour
- Caffeine lives here for launch (e.g. 100–150 mg in a ~10 g pack, under 5% caffeine, max 200 mg/day from the product as sports food)
- Fine grind, survives a warm bottle
- Sodium in Flood covers the bottle. Chamber B covers mid-session top-up and people who will not drink the whole bottle.

### 3. Reload (home, optional SKU)
- Daily creatine puck (~5 g)
- Not carried to the gym

## Session
1. Fill bottle → tear Flood in
2. Walk in → caffeine already in the bottle (or pellet if added later)
3. First heavy set → sniff Chamber A
4. Mid-session / hot room / cramp risk → one chew from Chamber B
5. After → finish bottle. No second powder pack required.

## What we rejected
- Protein vape / inhaling whey
- Putting Flood gel inside a slim pen
- Mixing ammonia into the drink or tank
- Dummy micro-doses of citrulline in a 2 ml cartridge
- Open gumball-style chew hopper (hygiene + gym brand)
- Caffeine in both Flood and the chew at launch (dose tracking dies)

## Australia sell path
- Flood = formulated supplementary sports food (powder, not capsules)
- Stick Chamber A = smelling salts with poison/warning labelling (already sold in AU sports stores)
- Stick Chamber B refill = formulated supplementary sports food / confectionery-format electrolyte tablet, sold as a sealed sleeve
- Do not market as inhaled medicine or lung pre-workout
- Soft claims: convenience kit, not “clinically proven 1RM”
- Tablets/capsules + performance claims = TGA / ARTG. Keep chew claims to electrolyte replacement / hydration convenience.
- No WADA-listed junk, no DMAA, no nicotine vape
- Empty Stim Stick can ship as a device. Food label lives on Flood packs and chew sleeves.

## Channel
- First: Brisbane strength gyms / boxes, consignment counter tray
- Impulse $12–25 for the door kit (Stick + 1 Flood + 1 chew sleeve)
- Owner margin ~40–50%
- Powder-only SKU for clubs that will not touch ammonia (Flood + chew sleeve, no Stick)
- Chew sleeve as a standalone $6–9 impulse next to the till
- Big-box franchises later, if ever

## Why the chew chamber belongs on the Stick
Flood is pre-door. Chamber A is the walk-up snap. Neither helps once they are under the bar and will not mix another pack.
Electrolyte chews are the mid-set product that already sells in AU cycling shops (SaltStick, Precision tabs) and almost never sits at a strength-gym door.
Bike bar-end dispensers exist. A gym-pocket two-chamber stick does not.
