# Gym Stim — invention brief for another model

Read this as source of truth. Do not revive killed ideas. Do not turn the stick into a vape, a slim citrulline pen, or a bundled food+poison kit.

Owner: Damon / swordofjustice76. SEQ, Australia. Personal product (not the Quokka Clean / After Close cash book). Repo: github.com/swordofjustice76/gym-stim

Status: parked concept, locked architecture. Hardware-first. Food refills later.

---

## One sentence

A reloadable aluminium gym stick that carries smelling salts, electrolyte chews, and a few days of *the athlete’s own* pre-workout powder in one pocket object. The stick is the product. Powder is BYO.

---

## Problem

Pre-workout is a tub + scoop + shaker + wait. Salts are a separate bottle. Chews are a third pack. People already own a water bottle and already bought a tub. Nothing exists that is: pocket aluminium, three materials, refillable from their tub, legal to sell in Australia as a standalone device.

Not solving: protein, creatine at the gym, mixing a drink *inside* the stick, inhaling powder.

## Elite hardware (locked)

**Body:** 38 mm diameter × 190 mm long. 6061-T6, type II anodise, pocket clip. Loaded ~140–180 g. Fat flashlight, not a vape pen.

Stack from the cap down:

1. **Chamber A — Snap.** Twist cap. 3–4 ml well. Smelling salt + menthol. Dry / unactivated so it can travel. Poison/warning mark on the cap. Factory refill caps only. Users do not homebrew ammonia.
2. **Chamber B — Chew.** Replaceable magazine. Ten Fastchew-format tabs (~16 mm × 5–6 mm). ~55 mm stack, 18 mm bore. One-handed click into the palm. Caffeine-free. Optional — stick works with B empty.
3. **Chamber C — Flask.** Screw-off 70 ml wide-mouth flask. Fill lines at 20 / 45 / 70 ml. Dump hatch tips into *their* water bottle. Desiccant in the lid. Funnel in the box. One flask, not four tiny cells.

**Why 70 ml:** typical tub scoops are 6–15 g. Fluffy powder ~0.50–0.60 g/ml. 70 ml ≈ three average scoops or four small C4 scoops. A 25 g “serious” scoop only fits two — print that on the flask. Do not chase four monster serves.

**Why not slimmer:** four real powder serves will not fit a slim pen. 30 × 150 only works if C is pressed pucks. User wants powder (dissolves better) and BYO tub, so 38 × 190 is the body.

**Why not four factory powder cells:** nobody will portion Ghost into 17 mm barrels on a Sunday. C must be one wide flask.

---

## Session

Sunday: unscrew C, scoop their tub to a line (or tear a Flood sachet in if they have no tub). Wipe dry first.

At the gym: tip C into the bottle they already carry. Heavy set → snap A. Mid-session → one chew from B.

Empty C → refill from the tub. Empty A → buy a salt cap. Empty B → buy a chew sleeve.

---

## AU launch law (non-negotiable)

Sell the **empty stick** first. One barcode, one regime: consumer goods / sports accessory.

Do **not** ship in the launch box: charged salts, pre-loaded chews, a Flood sachet. That kit mixes three laws:

- Empty body = consumer good
- Smelling salts = Poisons Standard / warning goods (ammonia scheduled by concentration). Already sold in AU as their own SKU
- Flood + chews = FSANZ Standard 2.9.4 formulated supplementary sports food (prescribed name, not for under-15s or pregnant, NIP, directions, one-day quantity)
- Tablets + performance claims = TGA / ARTG risk

**Launch box:** empty Elite body, empty C flask, funnel, desiccant puck, wipe card, fill-from-your-tub instructions. Claims stay dumb: carries powder, clip, cap well, chew magazine. No “inhaled pre-workout.” No 1RM language.

**Refills later, separate SKUs, separate labels, customer assembles:**

- Salt cap — own warning label
- Chew sleeve — own 2.9.4 label
- Flood sachet — own 2.9.4 label (optional fill for people with no tub; gym-desk SKU)

Compatible-with list on the card. Gym can sit the empty stick next to Ward/Zone salts and SaltStick chews until branded refills exist. Daily carton never shares a pack with a salt cap.

This is not legal advice. It is the product structure that keeps FSANZ and the TGA off the same hang-sell.

## Consumable spine (later)

One Flood formula, two packages: sachet for Daily / no-tub buyers; they may pour a sachet into C. Do not invent a second pre-workout.

Chews: one caffeine-free tablet, one diameter, one launch flavour (citrus or watermelon). Elite loads the magazine. Daily sells the sleeve.

Reload creatine puck is home-only and not required to launch.

Caffeine lives in Flood (or in *their* tub), not in the chew, not in the salt.

Scale story if it works: local SEQ doors → QLD → national AU target. International only after national, and only unactivated salt caps + local food. Do not mail live ammonia.

- Caffeine in both Flood and the chew at launch
- Plastic “lite Elite” that still has ammonia
- Two Flood formulas at launch
- Bundled launch kit: charged salts + food + device on one GTIN
- Performance / 1RM / medicine claims
- WADA junk, DMAA
- Daily as a mild Elite
- Making branded Flood mandatory for the stick to function
- Puck-only C unless powder flask tooling fails — user wants dissolve + BYO tub

## What to do if asked to design, write copy, or spec tooling

1. Keep Elite a container. C unscrews. Their tub is the default fill.
2. Keep launch box empty of salts, chews, and Flood.
3. Keep A / B / C as specified. Change millimetres only with a volume calculation, not vibes.
4. Daily is a later food carton, not a second metal stick.
5. Soft claims. Compatibility language, not protocol language.
6. If you draft a sell sheet: strength owner first. No salt hero shot on anything aimed at a chain.
7. If you draft a BOM: body, cap well, B insert, C flask, clip, funnel, desiccant. Food is a different PO.
8. If you model money: stick COGS is China; gym take ~40%; profit is refills after the metal is sold.
