# Gym Stim — modular product lock
14 September 2026. Sold as **one tube, swap the guts.** Not three tubes the user screws together.

Envelope stays **44 mm OD × 175 mm**, 6061-T6. C thread and A bayonet never change.

This file overrides the “B is only a chew clicker” sell. Mechanism detail for Pack D remains in `gym-stim-B-redesign.md`. Fit rules: `gym-stim-chamber-fit.md` (local / this repo when present). C dump: `gym-stim-C-redesign.md`.

---

## Who it is for

Convenience device for **gym-goers, pre and during the session.** One hand, clipped, no locker trip. Not an endurance toptube bag.

## Architecture

One aluminium spine. Three bays. Payloads are modules.

```text
A  22 mm   bayonet puck          sniff  |  chalk  |  (lid for Pack AB)
B  40 mm   keyed pack            Pack D |  Pack T |  (occupied by Pack AB)
C  ~95 mm  three 32 ml cells     powder only
```

Do not sell an A stub, a B mid, and a C flask as separate bodies.

---

## Chamber C — always powder

Lock. Dump door, selector, paddle. Three BYO cells.

- No storage.
- No chews.
- No earbuds.
- Fill from the top with C off. Dump one cell over the bottle.

Flood (owner pre) is not in production. Launch C is empty. User scoops their tub.

---

## Chamber A — two pucks, one latch

| Puck | Job |
|---|---|
| Sniff | 3–4 ml NH3 well. Elite default. Body never holds ammonia. |
| Chalk | 32 × 12 mm MgCO3 wafer. Later SKU. |
| Blank | Closed cap when they want neither. |

Do not mix food, plugs, or chews in a well that ever held ammonia. Swap the puck. Wipe the latch.

---

## Chamber B — one bay, two packs

Same 40 mm envelope. Same extract path (C unscrews, pack slides out). Shell does not know the flavour.

### Pack D — chew dispenser

Isolated dual-well shuttle. 4+4. Ring I/II. One button, one hard disc.

- Disc band: **Ø17.4–18.4 × 5.0–6.0 mm** (nominal 18.0 × 5.5).
- Named fit until calipered: Fastchew-class electrolyte discs.
- BYO: electrolyte / glucose / caffeine **only if the disc sits in the band**.
- Official Gym Stim chew sleeves later, one formula per pack. Caffeine sleeve only after ARTG.
- **Not** Hydralyte (those dissolve in 200 ml — bottle or C).
- **Not** gummies, gum, Revvies strips, Nuun, Dex4, capsules.

Detail: `gym-stim-B-redesign.md`, `gym-stim-B-multipurpose.md`.

### Pack T — dry tin

Spring or bayonet lid. ~35 ml. Thumb out, shut.

In:

- Wrapped caffeine gum / Revvies 5-packs
- Hard mints
- Gummies they pick by hand (accept slump in heat)
- Foam earplugs (not AirPods)
- Hair ties, ring, folded card

Out:

- Loose powder (C’s job)
- Ammonia
- A cascade window that claims to pour one gummy

### Pack AB — A+B as one storage well

Single liner that fills the B column and uses the **A bayonet as the lid**.

- Depth ~60 mm. Volume ~50–55 ml.
- No sniff. No chalk. No Pack D/T while AB is in.
- Same 44 × 175 body. No second forging.
- Flask config: storage nose + powder tail.

When they want salts again: AB out, puck + D or T back in.
